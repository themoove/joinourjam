On the record

What we do.

The tour plan is business development with you. None of this sits hidden inside the monthly fee, and none of it is a promise.

01

The tour plan

Strategy, optimisation, and the plan you keep. Fan region first, tour destination second. A list of dates is not the plan.

02

Socials and the release

The posts, the pitch, and the terms around the release, when that work is on. Presence and Bespoke. Both still proposed.

03

Sync and licensing

Licence requests for music you own. Separate from the monthly fee, and not assumed.

04

Your catalogue

Songs you have already made, put to work. A sale, if it happens, is its own piece of work. Not a promise.

05

Splits and registrations

Who owns what, written down, and the songs registered. You keep the music.

06

The tour deal

Dates, fees, and terms, from the fan regions out. When CITY JAM does this, CITY JAM receives 25% of the tour revenue. Outside the monthly fee. Not assumed.

When CITY JAM negotiates a tour, CITY JAM receives 25% of the tour revenue. That fee is not part of the monthly membership, and it is not assumed. If we cannot do the work, we would rather say so.
